Team Office Mail

A personal tool by Takaharu Suzuki

What it is

Team Office Mail is a personal tool built and used only by Takaharu Suzuki. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it does

It shows the owner's own Gmail inbox on his private home dashboard, and lets him archive, label, trash, block senders, unsubscribe from mailing lists, and send replies. Every change is made only after the owner reviews it and presses a button. It uses the Gmail API with the smallest set of permissions needed, and never permanently deletes email.
