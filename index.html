Team Office Calendar

A personal tool by Takaharu Suzuki

What it is

Team Office Calendar is a personal tool built and used only by Takaharu Suzuki. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it does

It shows the owner's Google Calendar events in month, week and day views, and lets the owner create, change and delete his own events. It uses the Google Calendar API with access limited to calendar events and the calendar list.
